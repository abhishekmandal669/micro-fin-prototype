Three ways to get funds, one portal

Every scheme runs through the same simple journey — apply, verify KYC, get your eligibility, and track disbursement.

🪙

Gold Loan

Pledge your gold jewellery or coins and get funds the same day. Minimal paperwork, purely KYC-based.

  • Interest from 9.5% p.a.
  • Loan-to-value up to 75%
  • Tenure 3 – 24 months
  • Disbursal within hours
🏠

Loan Against Property

Mortgage residential or commercial property for larger amounts and longer tenures at lower rates.

  • Interest from 10.5% p.a.
  • Loan-to-value up to 60%
  • Tenure up to 15 years
  • Amount ₹5L – ₹5Cr
💳

Personal Loan

No collateral needed. Get funds based on your income, employment and credit score.

  • Interest from 11% p.a.
  • Amount up to ₹15L
  • Tenure 12 – 60 months
  • Approval credit-score based
